# HECATE — principles

HECATE inherits the standard's principles (§2) unchanged and in the standard's order. Where two rules conflict and neither tier nor level decides, this order does. P-01 and P-02 always come first.

| # | Principle | What it means for HECATE |
|---|---|---|
| P-01 | Clarity over cleverness | A task's key, title, status and what blocks it are readable at a glance; no decoration competes with them |
| P-02 | Accessible by default | Every view works by keyboard, by touch and with a screen reader, in light and dark |
| P-03 | Design for the reading time | Today is read in under a minute on a phone; the Board is worked at a desk |
| P-04 | Restraint in colour | Torch amber is the one accent. Status colours mean status and nothing else |
| P-05 | Consistency | The app and Claude read the same views (`@hecate/core`), so they say the same thing |
| P-06 | Invisible when working | Saving and syncing show only when something needs attention |
| P-07 | Content first | Tasks, plans and notes, not chrome |
| P-08 | Progressive disclosure | Cards show what decides the next action; the editor holds the rest |
| P-09 | Build just enough, just in time | Tokens first, a component on its second use, a pattern on its third |
| P-10 | Decisions are documented | `DECISIONS.md`, at the time they are made |
| P-11 | Prefer the platform | System fonts, native form controls, headless primitives |
| P-12 | Scalable over bespoke | Tokens and shared components over one-off styling |
